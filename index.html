Who We Are

One DJ. Every Occasion. Zero Off Nights.

SP Entertainment LLC is a mobile DJ and event company built on one simple promise: read the room, play for the room, and never let the energy drop. From first dances to last calls, we handle the sound, the lighting and the flow so you can stay in the moment.
