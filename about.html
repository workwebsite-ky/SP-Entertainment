Put Some Respect
On His Name

SP Entertainment, founded by the esteemed DJ Stevie Steve, is a distinguished mobile and event DJ company that has served the tri-state area with unparalleled expertise for over four decades. Known for electrifying diverse crowds and catering to every musical taste, Stevie has built a reputation for professionalism, versatility and performances people remember.

SP Entertainment crafts bespoke experiences for weddings, private gatherings, corporate functions, clubs, concerts, festivals and community celebrations — all built to captivate the room and make the night unforgettable. Since its start in 1985, the mission hasn't changed: high-caliber performances and lasting memories, made through music.

Meet The Founder

DJ Stevie Steve

Stevie Pugh is the founder and the man behind the decks at SP Entertainment. Across a career spanning four decades, he's spun on national radio and on the high seas — and he brings that same command of a room to every event he plays.

  • National radio DJ on Majic 107.5 / 97.5 in Atlanta, featured on the "Jesus & Coffee" program.
  • Cruise-line performer for Royal Caribbean and Carnival — playing to crowds from every walk of life.
  • Every genre, every generation — old school, R&B, hip-hop and current hits, read to the room in real time.
