Everything For The Night

One team for the whole event — DJ, MC, sound and full production. Here's everything SP Entertainment brings to the floor.

Weddings

From the ceremony processional to the last dance, we run your timeline like professionals — smooth transitions, clean announcements and a floor that stays full. Your Coatesville-area wedding DJ for a night nobody forgets.

Birthday Events

Sweet 16s, milestone birthdays and everything between. We build the playlist around the guest of honor and keep the energy climbing all night long.

Private Events

Corporate mixers, anniversaries, reunions and house parties. Discreet, professional and dialed to your crowd — whatever the occasion calls for.

MC & Hosting

Need a voice to keep things moving? We handle introductions, announcements and crowd hype so your event flows without the awkward gaps.

Professional Sound

Crisp, powerful, room-filling audio with backup gear on hand. Indoor or outdoor, small room or big hall — the sound is tuned so every word and every drop lands clean.

Old School Sets

Motown, funk, disco, classic hip-hop and R&B — the timeless records that get every generation on their feet. A specialty of the house.

Uplighting

Wash your venue in the color of the night. Uplighting instantly upgrades any room and photographs beautifully — set to match your theme.

Cloud Machine

That dreamy low-lying cloud for first dances and grand entrances. A dramatic effect that turns a moment into a memory, safely and cleanly.

360 Photo Booth

The share-worthy centerpiece of the night. Guests step on, we capture the slow-motion spin, and your event travels across every feed.

Baby Showers

A warm, family-friendly atmosphere with the right music at the right volume. Celebrate the new arrival with a soundtrack everyone enjoys.

Grand Openings

Draw a crowd and hold their attention. Big sound and a polished presence give your launch the energy of a real event, not just an announcement.
